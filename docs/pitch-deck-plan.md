## Decisions still open

- **Capture cycle.** Slides 16A (about 5 s) and 16B (about 20 s) are variants: keep one, delete the other. The design target is 1 s, but the teammate's ESP32-CAM can't reach it yet.
- **Prices.** Every price is a placeholder: tiers of ₱1,500, ₱3,000 and ₱5,000; setup fee ₱3,500; kit ₱1,200; guard laptop ₱25,000; internet ₱1,500 a month. Check them against current Iloilo City prices and local HOA dues.
- **Founder stipends.** ₱5,000 per founder a month for 3 founders; the team size is not decided.
- **Growth rate.** The breakeven chart assumes 2 new subdivisions a month.

## Cost model behind slides 18–19

- Fixed costs are ₱21,800 a month:

  | Item | Per month |
  |---|---|
  | Founder stipends | ₱15,000 |
  | Marketing | ₱3,000 |
  | Cloud hosting | ₱1,500 |
  | Business registration | ₱1,000 |
  | NPC registration and Data Privacy Act compliance | ₱1,000 |
  | Domain and email | ₱300 |

- Variable costs per subdivision are ₱2,700 one-time (₱1,200 kit and ₱1,500 install) plus ₱400 a month (₱100 storage and ₱300 support).
- A tier breaks even at ₱21,800 ÷ (price − ₱400) subdivisions:

  | Tier | Subdivisions | Breakeven month |
  |---|---|---|
  | High | 5 | 3 |
  | Medium | 9 | 5 |
  | Low | 20 | 10 |

- Year 1 costs ₱261,600 fixed plus ₱127,200 variable, about ₱388,800 in total.
- The model leaves out the guard laptop (the HOA buys it) and the printed demo plates.
